Mochi · prototype · sample data
App structure
  • Five tabs: Overview (real cash, card cycles, what to sell) · Inventory · Stats (profit, cash flow, transactions by year/month) · Wallet (wallets, points, card debt, loan book) · Settings.
  • "Real cash" = cash + marketplace wallets − all card debt, including cards in someone else’s name, since you still transfer the money. Points sit in their own wallet and never count as cash.
  • Cards are assumed to close at month end and be paid the following month (26 / 27 / 4 in the sample data).
Configurable
  • Sales channels: one row per way to sell, paid in CASH (% fee) or POINTS (% bonus) — MapCamera cash and MapCamera Point are two rows alongside Mercari.
  • Language (English / Tiếng Việt), currency ¥/$/€/₫, owners, sales channels — in Settings. Owners live in the Wallet tab. Item categories (each with its own attribute checklist) are managed from the Inventory tab. Wallets and credit cards are created in the Wallet tab, next to the balances they affect.
  • Tweaks: VND rate, default shipping fee.
Things to try
  • Inventory → tap an item → Sell plan: pick a channel, edit price/shipping/point % in place, or enter a target profit → tap a suggested price to apply → one-tap sell, with money or points landing in the right wallet.
  • Item detail → Copy prompt for AI listing; Inventory → + Add item (custom attributes) / Import JSON.
  • Wallet → Move money between wallets; Overview → mark a transfer to a card owner as done.
