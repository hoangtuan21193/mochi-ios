Phrases that work on every iPhone (iOS 17+) How much do I have in Mochi · What do I pay next in Mochi · Add a spend in Mochi · Scan a receipt in Mochi · Buy advice in Mochi · Who owes me in Mochi For automations, in the Shortcuts app: Add an Apple Pay spend (Wallet: Amount, Merchant, Card; Ask me on or off) and Read a card email (Email: Subject, Body). Also in Spotlight, the Shortcuts app and the Action button. The phrase has to name Mochi; that is how Siri knows which app answers.
Kept private Money figures ask for Face ID when the iPhone is locked, so nobody at your desk hears your balance. Adding a spend works locked and waits in the review list. Free-form questions (“eating out last month?”) go through Ask Mochi; Siri hands them over with Apple Intelligence where it’s on.
