How a receipt becomes a transaction, without a network
Six steps in a row; four other ways in join where they fit. Everything in the green band runs on the iPhone.
On this iPhone · works in airplane mode
1 · Capture
VisionKit document camera finds the paper edges and shoots by itself
2 · Read the text
Vision text recognition, Japanese and English, about half a second
3 · Understand
Rules find 合計, 日付, 登録番号 T…, card ****1234. The on-device model adds lines and a category
4 · Fill from memory
Shop by T-number or phone, card by its last 4, the category you chose there last time
5 · Check
One sheet. Sure fields are filled in; unsure ones say “check”
6 · Save
A transaction, plus a belonging when a line is worth tracking
Photo or screenshot
A receipt already in Photos, a PayPay or Amazon order screen
Apple Pay tap
A Shortcuts “Transaction” automation hands over amount, shop and card
Where you are
Location when the app opens, matched to places you saved before. No map lookup
Card statement CSV
Downloaded from the card’s own site; matched, repeats skipped
Two levels of “understand”
Every iPhone: rules alone fill total, date, time, shop and card from a Japanese receipt.
iPhone 15 Pro or later with Apple Intelligence on: Apple’s on-device Foundation Models also splits the lines and suggests a category. Nothing leaves the phone either way.
Time budget: about five seconds
Capture 1 s · read 0.5 s · understand 0.3 s · fill 0.1 s · you glance and tap Save, about 3 s.
The photo is kept only if you ask, and only on this iPhone.
